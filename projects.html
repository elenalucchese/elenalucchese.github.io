Workforce · wellbeing

Burnout among emergency workers in Liguria

For the Liguria Region · ProQOL survey of doctors, nurses, rescuers and dispatch operators

Which emergency workers are most at risk of burnout and secondary traumatic stress? I analysed the Professional Quality of Life (ProQOL) survey of the regional emergency system, covering physicians, nurses, rescuers and drivers, and dispatch operators, both employees and volunteers.

So whatPrevention should target clinical staff and experienced workers first, with dedicated support for secondary trauma.

Workforce · staffing models

Employees or contractors in the emergency department?

Niguarda Hospital, Milan · with D. Battiston and J. Blanes i Vidal · under review

Hospitals increasingly fill emergency department shifts with contract doctors. Patients are assigned to doctors almost at random, which allows a fair comparison of the two groups on the same kind of patients.

Productivity over the course of a shift

Dispatch · caller location

The hidden cost of not knowing where the caller is

Italian Economic Journal, 2025 · paper

Dispatchers often receive imprecise addresses. Comparing the outbound trip (destination uncertain) with the return trip (destination known), on Liguria emergency data, isolates the time lost looking for the patient.

So whatBetter caller location (handset location, address validation, dispatcher protocols) is one of the cheapest ways to save minutes.

Response times · outcomes

What one minute of ambulance delay costs

Health Economics 33(4), 2024 · paper

Rain slows ambulances for reasons unrelated to the patient's condition. Using rainfall as a natural experiment, I estimate the causal effect of response time on mortality from cardiac events.
